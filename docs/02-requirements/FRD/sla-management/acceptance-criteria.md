# Acceptance criteria — SLA management (REQ-SUP-002)

Each criterion maps to at least one test case in `test-cases/functional/sla-management/` ([TESTPLAN-SUP-002](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TESTPLAN-SUP-002.md)).

| ID | Requirement | Criterion | Test case |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | .1, .2, .5 | **Given** a customer creates a ticket **then** it has a first-response deadline and a later resolution deadline counted from its creation time and no first response yet; **given** the configuration **then** it lists LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH and URGENT, with URGENT around the clock. | [TC-SUP-006](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-006.md) |
| AC-2 | .3, .4 | **Given** business hours Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00 Asia/Kolkata **when** a deadline is calculated **then** 240 minutes from Monday 10:00 is Monday 14:00; 240 minutes from Friday 16:00 is Monday 11:00; a ticket created on Saturday noon, before opening or at closing time starts at the next opening; 1440 minutes from Monday 09:00 is Wednesday 15:00; an around-the-clock target ignores business hours; business hours with no day or a start after the end are refused. | [TC-SUP-007](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-007.md) |
| AC-3 | .8 | **Given** a MEDIUM ticket **when** its priority becomes URGENT **then** its first-response deadline is 60 minutes and its resolution deadline 480 minutes after the **creation** time, and the first-response deadline has moved. | [TC-SUP-008](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-008.md) |
| AC-4 | .6, .7 | **Given** an OPEN ticket **when** it is only categorized **then** there is no first response; **when** it is assigned **then** the first response is stored; **when** it is then escalated **then** the first response time does not change; **given** another ticket resolved straight away **then** its first response is stored and its resolution state is MET. | [TC-SUP-009](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-009.md) |
| AC-5 | .9, .11 | **Given** an assigned URGENT ticket and an alert list **when** 100 minutes have passed **then** no warning exists; **when** 400 minutes have passed (80% of 480 is 384) **then** the resolution warning is stored once, the ticket is not escalated, the assignee and the listed person get an in-app notice "Support ticket nearing its resolution target" and the state is WARNING; **when** the check runs again **then** nobody is told again; the warning point is 80% of the target (48 minutes of a 60-minute 24x7 target). | [TC-SUP-010](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-010.md) |
| AC-6 | .10, .6 | **Given** an OPEN URGENT ticket **when** 70 minutes have passed without a response **then** the first-response breach is flagged, the ticket is ESCALATED, its priority is still URGENT, no first response is recorded, the listed person is told "…breached its first response target" and the state is BREACHED; **when** a person then assigns it **then** the response is recorded and the state is MET_LATE. | [TC-SUP-011](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-011.md) |
| AC-7 | .10 | **Given** a resolved ticket **when** the check runs three days later **then** nothing is breached and its status stays RESOLVED. | [TC-SUP-012](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-012.md) |
| AC-8 | .12 | **Given** a platform administrator **when** saving targets and settings **then** a first-response target longer than the resolution target, an unknown priority, no business day, a start after the end, an unknown time zone, a warning point of 100 and an invalid email address are each refused; valid settings are saved with the alert addresses in lowercase and without duplicates; the screen shows the durations in readable form, blocks the same errors before sending, and shows the server's error. | [TC-SUP-013](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-013.md) |
| AC-9 | .12 | **Given** an open ticket with deadlines **when** the targets are saved **then** the ticket's deadlines do not change; **when** a ticket is then created or re-prioritised **then** the new targets apply. | [TC-SUP-014](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-014.md) |
| AC-10 | .13 | **Given** a customer opens their own ticket **then** the deadlines are shown ("we aim to respond by … and resolve by …", only the resolution once someone has responded, none for a resolved or closed ticket or a ticket without deadlines) and the SLA states are absent; **given** an administrator opens it **then** both states are ON_TRACK. | [TC-SUP-015](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-015.md) |
| AC-11 | .13 | **Given** the Support tickets screen **then** each row shows a first-response chip and a resolution chip with their due time and a breach flag; the SLA filter shows only warning or breached tickets and an empty state when none matches. | [TC-SUP-016](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-016.md) |
| AC-12 | .14 | **Given** a ticket created before SLA tracking (no deadlines) **then** it has no chips and no states, the check never touches it and a priority change does not give it deadlines. | [TC-SUP-017](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-017.md) |
| AC-13 | .12 | **Given** a user without `MANAGE_SUPPORT_TICKETS` **when** calling `/admin/support/sla` (GET) or its two PUT endpoints **then** 403 (401 without a token); **given** an administrator **then** 200. | [TC-SUP-018](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-018.md) |
| AC-14 | .9, .10, .12 | **Given** saved targets, saved settings, a warning and a breach **then** the audit log has `SLA_POLICY_UPDATED`, `SLA_SETTINGS_UPDATED`, `TICKET_SLA_WARNING` and `TICKET_SLA_BREACHED`. | [TC-SUP-019](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-019.md) |
| AC-15 | Regression | **Given** the ticket actions of REQ-SUP-001 (create, assign, escalate, resolve, close, ownership) **then** they behave as before, with the SLA fields added. | [TC-SUP-020](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-020.md) (re-runs [TC-SUP-001](../../../../test-cases/functional/ticket-management/TC-SUP-001.md) to [TC-SUP-005](../../../../test-cases/functional/ticket-management/TC-SUP-005.md)) |
| AC-16 | Accessibility, i18n | **Given** the SLA settings screen, the SLA chips and My tickets **then** each passes the axe test and its text exists in `en.json` and `es.json`. | [TC-SUP-013](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-013.md), [TC-SUP-015](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-015.md), [TC-SUP-016](../../../../test-cases/functional/sla-management/TC-SUP-016.md) |
