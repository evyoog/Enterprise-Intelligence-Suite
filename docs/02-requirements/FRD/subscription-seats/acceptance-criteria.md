# Acceptance criteria — Seats and quantity (REQ-SUB-003)

| ID | Requirement | Criterion | Test case |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | .1 | **Given** an organization subscription **then** its seat summary shows quantity ≥ 1, seats in use and the minimum; **given** an individual subscription **then** no seat control is offered and a seat change is refused. | [TC-SUB-013](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-013.md) |
| AC-2 | .2, .8 | **Given** 3 seats and 2 in use **when** a Manage billing user sets 8 **then** the quantity is 8 immediately, the organization seat limit rises to 8, and a 4th to 8th member can be added. | [TC-SUB-014](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-014.md) |
| AC-3 | .3 | **Given** 3 seats in use **when** the user sets 2 **then** it is refused with "3 seats are in use…" and the quantity is unchanged; setting 3 is accepted (as a decrease, see AC-10). | [TC-SUB-015](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-015.md) |
| AC-4 | .4 | **Given** all seats of a subscription in use **when** a member is added **then** it is refused as over the seat limit; **when** seats are added **then** the member who was left without a seat gets it at once. | [TC-SUB-016](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-016.md) |
| AC-5 | .5 | **Given** a seat change **then** an audit entry and a `SeatsChanged` event (from, to) exist. | [TC-SUB-014](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-014.md) |
| AC-6 | .10 | **Given** a member without Manage billing or another organization's subscription **when** changing seats **then** 403 / 404. | [TC-SUB-017](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-017.md) |
| AC-7 | Accessibility | **Given** the seats section and its confirmation dialog **then** each passes the axe test. | [TC-SUB-017](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-017.md) |
| AC-8 | .7 | **Given** an organization subscription on a Monthly plan priced 1000 per seat **then** the seat summary shows the price of one seat; a renewal with 3 seats is invoiced 3 × 1000 with quantity 3 on the line; an organization purchase is billed price × the seats it starts with. | [TC-SUB-045](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-045.md) |
| AC-9 | .8 | **Given** 15 days left of a 30-day term, 5 seats **when** the user sets 8 **then** the quantity is 8 at once, one invoice is created for 3 added seats × price × 15 ÷ 30 on a line "Added seats…", the answer says `effect: NOW` with the invoice id, and the screen shows the estimate before saving and a link to the invoices after. | [TC-SUB-046](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-046.md) |
| AC-10 | .9 | **Given** 5 seats **when** the user sets 3 **then** the quantity stays 5, `pendingQuantity` is 3, no invoice is created, the screen says "Falls to 3 seats on {date}. Nothing is credited."; **when** the user asks for 5 again **then** the pending decrease is withdrawn. | [TC-SUB-047](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-047.md) |
| AC-11 | .9 | **Given** a pending decrease to 3 **when** the subscription renews **then** the quantity is 3 and the renewal invoice bills 3 seats; **given** more seats in use than the pending value **then** the renewal keeps the seats in use. | [TC-SUB-048](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-048.md) |
| AC-12 | .10, .12 | **Given** a user with Manage subscriptions only **then** they see the seats and cannot change them (403; no controls); **given** a user with Manage billing **then** they can; **given** a subscription whose term has ended **then** a change is refused (409 "This term has ended…"). | [TC-SUB-049](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-049.md) |
| AC-13 | .8 | **Given** a seat increase **when** the invoice list is opened in Billing **then** the new invoice shows the line "Added seats: {product} — {plan}", its quantity and amount, and tax on it. | [TC-SUB-050](../../../../test-cases/functional/subscription-seats/TC-SUB-050.md) |
