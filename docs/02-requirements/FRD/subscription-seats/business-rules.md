# Business rules — Seats and quantity (REQ-SUB-003)

| ID | Rule | Enforced in | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-1 | Quantity is an integer from 1 to 100 000. Individual subscriptions are always 1; a seat change on one is refused (404: it is not an organization subscription). | backend | .1 |
| BR-2 | An **increase** takes effect at once (BR-9). A **decrease** does not: it is stored as a pending quantity and takes effect with the next renewal (BR-10). *Supersedes the C63 rule "a seat change takes effect at once; there is no pending state".* | backend | .2, .9 |
| BR-3 | New quantity ≥ seats in use; otherwise 409 "{inUse} seats are in use. Remove members before reducing seats." Seats in use are **named seats per product** ([C90](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c90)): the ACTIVE members who have that product ON. | backend | .3 |
| BR-4 | Adding an ACTIVE member needs a free seat under the organization's licensed seats and under the quantity of each ACTIVE subscription of the organization, unless the organization allows seat overage. | backend | .4 |
| BR-5 | Increasing above the organization's licensed seats raises them to the new quantity; decreasing (now or at renewal) leaves them unchanged. | backend | .4 (default) |
| BR-6 | Only ACTIVE or SUSPENDED organization subscriptions of the caller's own organization can be changed; another organization's subscription is 404. | backend | .2 |
| BR-7 | Each change writes the audit entry `SUBSCRIPTION_SEATS_CHANGED` (from → to) and publishes `SeatsChanged` in the same transaction. A scheduled decrease writes `SUBSCRIPTION_SEATS_DECREASE_SCHEDULED` and a withdrawal `SUBSCRIPTION_SEATS_DECREASE_WITHDRAWN`; no `SeatsChanged` is published until the quantity actually changes. | backend | .5 |
| BR-8 | **Price per seat** (engineering default, to confirm): for an organization subscription an invoice line bills the plan price × the quantity, with the quantity on the line. An individual subscription is billed one price. New purchases are billed for the seats the organization already has on the product, else its licensed seats (at least the active members, at least 1). | backend | .7 |
| BR-9 | **Increase:** the quantity changes at once and a separate invoice is created for the added seats only: added seats × seat price × remaining days ÷ days in the term, rounded to the smallest currency unit, taxed like any invoice. Days in the term: 30 (Monthly), 365 (Yearly), else the length of the current term. Remaining days are rounded up and capped at the days in the term. No invoice for a free plan or when no days remain. A coupon is not applied. | backend | .8 |
| BR-10 | **Decrease:** the quantity stays until the renewal; `pendingQuantity` holds the new value; **no invoice and no credit**. It is applied by the renewal itself (the auto-renew job, which also runs when auto-renew is turned back on during the grace period) before the renewal invoice is created, so the renewal bills the lower quantity. Organizations have no manual renew (see [Open points](requirement.md#open-points)). A subscription with no renewal date has no renewal to wait for, so the decrease applies at once. | backend | .9 |
| BR-11 | **Withdraw:** asking for the current quantity while a decrease is pending clears it (effect `WITHDRAWN`); asking for it with nothing pending changes nothing (effect `UNCHANGED`). | backend | .9 |
| BR-12 | **At renewal** the new quantity is the larger of the pending value, the seats in use at that moment and 1. If seats are in use above the pending value the renewal keeps them, and the audit entry says so. | backend | .9 |
| BR-13 | **Permission:** changing seats needs the organization permission `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION_BILLING`. Listing the organization's subscriptions and reading a seat summary need `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION_BILLING` or `MANAGE_SUBSCRIPTIONS`. Any other member: 403. Organization administrators hold Manage billing by default. | backend | .10 |
| BR-14 | A seat change is refused (409) once the subscription's term has ended, including during the read-only grace period; the customer renews first. | backend | .12 |
| BR-15 | A seat change is never an order: it needs no approval and creates no order. | backend | .11 |
