# Workflow — Offering management

1. The administrator opens **Offerings** (Platform area). The page shows the offerings and a note that prices stay on the products.
2. **New offering:** name, description, status (DRAFT by default), the products to include. Save.
3. **Publish:** change the status to ACTIVE (needs an ACTIVE product). Customers now see it under *Offerings*.
4. **Retire:** change the status to RETIRED; it disappears from the public list. A DRAFT offering can be deleted.
5. **Product rules** tab: for each product choose who can buy it (individuals and organizations / individuals only / organizations only) and tick the products it works with. Save.
6. A customer opens a product: the Overview shows *Works with*. Adding an `ORGANIZATION` product to the cart as an individual shows *not available for your account type* and blocks checkout until it is removed.
