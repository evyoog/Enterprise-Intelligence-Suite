# Acceptance criteria — Product evaluation (REQ-MKT-005)

Each criterion maps to at least one test case in `test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/` ([TESTPLAN-MKT-005](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TESTPLAN-MKT-005.md)). TC-MKT-049 to TC-MKT-071 follow the criteria in order (TC number = 48 + the AC number).

| ID | Requirement | Criterion | Test case |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | .1, .14, BR-EVL-001, BR-EVL-002 | **Given** a platform administrator editing a product **when** they set **Free trial (days)** to a number from 1 to 90 **then** it is saved and returned as `trialDays`; **when** they leave it empty **then** the product offers no trial; **when** they enter 0 or 91 **then** the save is refused (400); the product form shows the field with a hint and its i18n text exists in `en.json` and `es.json`. | [TC-MKT-049](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-049.md) |
| AC-2 | .2, .7, BR-EVL-003, BR-EVL-006, BR-EVL-010 | **Given** a product with 7 trial days and two plans **when** a signed-in individual starts a trial **then** a subscription exists with `trial` true, status ACTIVE, on the higher-priced plan, 1 seat, `autoRenew` false, ending about 7 days later, no invoice, and the customer is told "Free trial started". | [TC-MKT-050](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-050.md) |
| AC-3 | .4, BR-EVL-003 to BR-EVL-005 | **Given** a customer who already started a trial of a product **when** they start another **then** it is refused (400 "already"); **given** a customer who subscribed before **then** a trial is refused; **given** a product with no trial days **then** it is refused (400 "does not offer"); **given** a product that is not published **then** 404. | [TC-MKT-051](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-051.md) |
| AC-4 | .3, .4, BR-EVL-006, BR-EVL-007 | **Given** an organization with 5 licensed seats, an administrator and an ordinary member **when** the member starts an organization trial **then** 403; **when** the administrator does **then** the trial has 5 seats; **when** the administrator starts a second trial of the same product **then** it is refused (400); a member with Manage billing may start one. | [TC-MKT-052](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-052.md) |
| AC-5 | .5, BR-EVL-008 | **Given** a started trial **then** the subscription has a service instance in status PROVISIONING and a `SubscriptionCreated` event carrying `trial: true` was written to the outbox with the subscription's fields, so the product can create the trial tenant; a non-trial subscription's events carry `trial: false`. | [TC-MKT-053](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-053.md) |
| AC-6 | .6, BR-EVL-009 | **Given** a running trial **when** the customer renews it, turns auto-renew on, or changes its plan **then** each is refused (400) with a message that says a free trial cannot do this; **when** an organization administrator changes the trial's seats or plan **then** each is refused (400). | [TC-MKT-054](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-054.md) |
| AC-7 | .7, BR-EVL-010, BR-EVL-011 | **Given** a trial that ends in 2 days **when** the hourly job runs twice **then** the owner gets exactly one "ends soon" notice. | [TC-MKT-055](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-055.md) |
| AC-8 | .8, BR-EVL-013, BR-EVL-017 | **Given** a trial whose end time has passed **when** the hourly job runs **then** the subscription is EXPIRED with no grace end date, the service instance is DEPROVISIONED, the owner is told "Free trial ended", `TRIAL_EXPIRED` is audited and a `SubscriptionChanged` event with status EXPIRED is published. | [TC-MKT-056](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-056.md) |
| AC-9 | .9, BR-EVL-015 | **Given** a running trial **when** the customer buys a paid plan (the service-level purchase) **then** the same subscription has `trial` false, is PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION until paid and has auto-renew on; **given** a free plan **then** it is ACTIVE at once; **given** an organization's direct purchase **then** `trial` is false too. | [TC-MKT-057](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-057.md) |
| AC-10 | .9, BR-EVL-016 | **Given** a customer whose trial has ended (EXPIRED) **when** they add a paid plan to the cart and check out **then** the purchase goes through and the subscription is a normal one; **given** a customer whose trial is **still running** **then** the intended result is that checkout works; **known gap:** today the cart reports `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED` and refuses checkout until the trial has ended. | [TC-MKT-058](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-058.md) |
| AC-11 | .10, BR-EVL-018, BR-EVL-019 | **Given** a signed-in customer and a published product **when** they request a demo with a preferred time and a message **then** a ticket exists in the category "Demo request" with the subject "Demo request: {product}", the time and message in its description, status OPEN and its SLA deadlines; **when** no product is chosen **then** 400; **when** the product does not exist **then** 404; **when** a field is over 1000 characters **then** 400. | [TC-MKT-059](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-059.md) |
| AC-12 | .12, .13, BR-EVL-022, BR-EVL-025 | **Given** two products with plans **when** anyone compares a plan of each for 3 seats **then** the response has 2 items in the order asked, each with its chosen plan, the subtotal price x 3 in the item's own currency (300.00 and 751.50 INR for 100.00 and 250.50) and the product's trial days. | [TC-MKT-060](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-060.md) |
| AC-13 | .12, BR-EVL-023, BR-EVL-024 | **Given** a product with several plans compared on its own **then** its cheapest plan is used; **given** a product without plans **then** it is shown at its flat price with no plan; **given** no `seats` **then** 1 seat is used. | [TC-MKT-061](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-061.md) |
| AC-14 | .13, BR-EVL-026, BR-EVL-027 | **Given** a visitor **then** each item's tax status is `NOT_SIGNED_IN`; **given** a signed-in customer without billing details **then** `NEEDS_BILLING_DETAILS`; **given** a signed-in customer with billing details and the EIS tax rules **then** `ESTIMATED` with the tax and the total (subtotal + tax); **given** the external tax provider on, or a sale the rules cannot tax **then** `AT_CHECKOUT`. | [TC-MKT-062](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-062.md) |
| AC-15 | .12, BR-EVL-022 to BR-EVL-024 | **Given** the compare call **then** no items, one item, five items, the same item twice, a part that is not a number, seats 0, and a plan of another product are each refused (400, with the reason); an unknown or unpublished product is 404. | [TC-MKT-063](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-063.md) |
| AC-16 | .3, .10, .12, BR-EVL-021 | **Given** no token **when** calling `GET /products/compare` **then** it is open (a bad request is 400 or 404, never 401) and it changes nothing; **when** calling `POST /me/trials`, `POST /organization/me/trials` or `POST /me/demo-requests` **then** 401; **given** an organization member without Manage billing **then** an organization trial is 403 (AC-4). | [TC-MKT-064](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-064.md) |
| AC-17 | .11, .15 | **Given** the product page **then** a "Try it first" panel shows the trial offer (days, no card, all features) with **Start free trial** when the product offers one, **Request a demo** and **Watch tutorials**; a signed-out visitor is asked to sign in first; a member who may not start the organization's trial is told so; a customer who already has or had a subscription is told the trial is not available; **Request a demo** opens a form with preferred time and message and confirms the ticket. | [TC-MKT-065](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-065.md) |
| AC-18 | .15, BR-EVL-006 | **Given** My subscriptions (and the organization's subscriptions) with a running trial **then** the subscription shows a **Free trial** chip with the days left and a **Subscribe** button; it offers no renew, auto-renew or plan change. | [TC-MKT-066](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-066.md) |
| AC-19 | .15 | **Given** the catalog **when** a customer ticks **Compare** on 2 to 4 cards **then** a tray shows the count and the chosen products with remove and Clear, and **Compare now** opens `/compare?items=…`; a fifth card cannot be ticked; with fewer than 2 chosen, Compare now is disabled. | [TC-MKT-067](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-067.md) |
| AC-20 | .12, .13, .15 | **Given** the page `/compare?items=…&seats=…` **then** it shows the items side by side (product, plan, billing period, price per seat, seats, subtotal, tax estimate or its status message, included features, trial days, a link to the product), lets the visitor change seats and the items (2 to 4), keeps them in the address, shows the estimate note and the loading, empty and error states, and is open without signing in. | [TC-MKT-068](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-068.md) |
| AC-21 | Regression, .6, BR-EVL-012 | **Given** an ACTIVE trial that ends within the renewal reminder window **when** the renewal reminder job runs **then** it sends no renewal reminder for it; **given** a normal subscription in the same window **then** it does. | [TC-MKT-069](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-069.md) |
| AC-22 | Regression | **Given** the subscription lifecycle, seats, renewal and expiry of a normal subscription (`trial` false) **then** they behave as before: renew, auto-renew, plan change, the 7-day grace period and expiry, the cart and the organization purchase. | [TC-MKT-070](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-070.md) |
| AC-23 | Accessibility, i18n | **Given** the "Try it first" panel and the demo dialog, the trial chip, the Compare toggle and tray and the compare page **then** each passes the axe test, every control is keyboard operable and its text exists in `en.json` and `es.json`. | [TC-MKT-071](../../../../test-cases/functional/product-evaluation/TC-MKT-071.md) |
