# Business rules — Product evaluation (REQ-MKT-005)

Decided in [C102](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c102) (TR-1 note, TR-2 A, 2026-10-15). Enforced by the backend; the cited class and method are the source of truth.

## Trials
| ID | Rule | Enforced in | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-EVL-001 | **A product offers a trial when `trialDays` is set**, a whole number from 1 to 90. Empty (null) = no trial; this is the default for every product, including those that existed before. A value outside 1 to 90 is refused on the product form's save (400) and by the database check `chk_products_trial_days`. Publishing or retiring a product keeps its trial days. | `ProductCreateRequest` (`@Min(1) @Max(90)`), `ProductService#applyShowcase`, database | .1, .14 |
| BR-EVL-002 | **A product update replaces every field**, so an update that leaves out `trialDays` clears the trial. | `ProductService#updateProduct` | .14 |
| BR-EVL-003 | **What can be tried.** The product must exist and be **ACTIVE** (else 404 "Product not found") and must offer a trial (else 400 "{product} does not offer a free trial."). | `SubscriptionService#requireTrialProduct` | .2, .4 |
| BR-EVL-004 | **Audience.** The product's audience rule is checked as for buying: an individual cannot trial an organization-only product and an organization cannot trial an individual-only one (409 "{product} is available to … only."). | `EligibilityService#assertEligible` | .4 |
| BR-EVL-005 | **One trial per owner and product, ever.** If the customer (or, for an organization trial, the organization) already has a subscription to the product, **in any status** (active, suspended, cancelled, expired, pending or a past trial), a trial is refused (400 "You already have, or had, a subscription to {product}, so a free trial is not available." / "Your organization already has, or had, …"). | `SubscriptionService#startTrial`, `#startOrganizationTrial` | .4 |
| BR-EVL-006 | **What a trial is.** A new `ProductSubscription` with `trial = true`, status **ACTIVE**, `startedAt` now, **`expiresAt` = now + `trialDays` days**, **`autoRenew = false`**, no grace date, on the **highest plan of the product by price** (`planId` null for a product without plans, whose single price covers everything). Seats: **1** for an individual; for an organization the **organization's seats** (the greater of its licensed seats and its ACTIVE members, at least 1, at most 100 000). **No invoice is generated and no payment is taken.** | `SubscriptionService#beginTrial`, `#highestPlanId`, `#initialSeats` | .2, .3 |
| BR-EVL-007 | **Who starts an organization trial.** The caller must be an ACTIVE member of an organization in good standing (else 404 "You are not a member of an organization" / 403) and an **organization administrator or hold `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION_BILLING`** (`OrderService#canPurchaseDirectly`), else 403 "You do not have permission to do this". The trial belongs to the caller's organization. After it starts, member product access is recalculated (`OrganizationEntitlementsChangedEvent`). | `TrialService#startForOrganization` | .3 |
| BR-EVL-008 | **Provisioning is the normal path.** Starting a trial publishes **`SubscriptionCreated`** (outbox, same transaction) with `subscriptionId`, `productId`, `planId`, `ownerType`, `ownerCustomerId`, `ownerOrganizationId`, `status`, `expiresAt` and **`trial`** (true), and creates the subscription's **service instance** (`PROVISIONING`). The product creates the tenant and its database; **EIS creates no database**. Every other subscription event carries `trial` too (false when it is not a trial). | `SubscriptionService#publish`, `ServiceInstanceService#syncFromSubscription` | .5 |
| BR-EVL-009 | **A trial cannot be renewed or changed.** Each of these is refused with 400 and a message that says to subscribe: **Renew** ("A free trial cannot be renewed. …"), **turn auto-renew on or off**, individual or organization ("A free trial does not renew. …"), **change plan**, individual or organization ("A free trial has all features on the highest plan. …"), and an organization's **seat change** ("A free trial has the organization's seats. Subscribe to change them."). Suspend, reactivate and cancel follow their usual rules. | `SubscriptionService#renewSubscription`, `#changeAutoRenew`, `#changePlan`, `#changeOrganizationPlan`; `SubscriptionSeatService#changeSeats` | .6 |
| BR-EVL-010 | **Notices to an individual owner** (category SUBSCRIPTION, in-app and email as usual): "Free trial started: {product}" with the end date; "Your free trial of {product} ends soon" **once**, when the trial ends within 3 days (`trial_reminded_at` is then set); "Free trial ended: {product}". An organization-owned subscription is **not notified** by the subscription feature, so an organization's trial sends none. | `SubscriptionService#beginTrial`, `#remindEndingTrials`, `#expireTrial`, `#notify` | .7 |
| BR-EVL-011 | **The 3-day notice** is sent by the hourly `SubscriptionExpiryJob`: it selects ACTIVE trials with no reminder yet whose `expiresAt` is between now and now + 3 days, marks each and sends once. A trial of 3 days or fewer is therefore reminded at the first run after it starts. | `SubscriptionService#remindEndingTrials` | .7 |
| BR-EVL-012 | **Trials are not renewal-reminder candidates.** `RenewalReminderService` skips every subscription with `trial = true`, so a trial never gets a renewal reminder; its own notice is BR-EVL-010. | `RenewalReminderService#sendDue` | .6, .7 |
| BR-EVL-013 | **A trial ends with no grace period.** The hourly job picks ACTIVE subscriptions with auto-renew off whose `expiresAt` has passed; a trial among them is set **EXPIRED at once** (no `graceEndsAt`, never read-only), the owner is notified, **`TRIAL_EXPIRED`** is audited, **`SubscriptionChanged` with status `EXPIRED`** is published, the **service instance becomes `DEPROVISIONED`** and an organization's member product access is recalculated. A trial can therefore end up to an hour after `expiresAt`. | `SubscriptionService#expireOverdueSubscriptions`, `#expireTrial` | .8 |
| BR-EVL-014 | **Trial data.** EIS only sends the deprovision event. **When the product deletes the trial data is the product's decision**; no time is set here. | contract ([REQ-ORD-002](../provisioning-contract/requirement.md)) | .8 |
| BR-EVL-015 | **Buying turns the trial off.** Each purchase path sets `trial = false`: `subscribe`, `subscribeFromCart` (an individual's checkout) and `activateOrganizationSubscription` (an organization's direct purchase or approved order). The same subscription row, and so the same service instance, carries on with the chosen plan, `expiresAt` = the plan's renewal date and auto-renew on. A **paid plan bought over a trial starts after payment** (status `PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION` until then), because a running trial does not count as an already active paid subscription. A free plan starts at once. | `SubscriptionService#subscribe`, `#subscribeFromCart`, `#activateOrganizationSubscription` | .9 |
| BR-EVL-016 | **Known gap: the cart refuses a running trial.** `CartService#issuesFor` reports `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED` for a subscription whose status is ACTIVE, which includes a running trial, and checkout is refused while any issue exists. A customer can therefore buy through the cart only after the trial has ended (status EXPIRED). The cart is not changed in this build. | `CartService#issuesFor`, `#hasActiveSubscription` | .9 |
| BR-EVL-017 | **Audit.** `TRIAL_STARTED` (actor: the customer; the organization id for an organization trial) and `TRIAL_EXPIRED`. Buying keeps its own entries (`SUBSCRIPTION_CREATED`). | `SubscriptionService#beginTrial`, `#expireTrial` | .5, .8 |

## Demo requests
| ID | Rule | Enforced in | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-EVL-018 | **A demo request is a support ticket.** It needs a signed-in customer and a published (ACTIVE) product: no product id = 400 "Choose a product."; an unknown or unpublished product = 404. The ticket has the **category "Demo request"**, the subject **"Demo request: {product name}"** (cut at 200 characters), and a description "I would like a demo of {product} (product {id})." followed by "Preferred time: …" and "Message: …" when given. It is OPEN with MEDIUM priority like any ticket, gets its **SLA deadlines** ([BR-SLA-005](../sla-management/business-rules.md)) and is audited (`TICKET_CREATED`). | `DemoRequestService#request`, `SupportTicketService#createTicket(…, category)` | .10 |
| BR-EVL-019 | **Free text is trimmed and limited.** "Preferred time" and "Message" are optional, trimmed, and at most **1000 characters** each (else 400 "Keep each field under 1000 characters."). A product need not offer a trial to be demoed. A customer may ask more than once; no limit exists. | `DemoRequestService#clean` | .10 |
| BR-EVL-020 | **The ticket is handled like any ticket.** It appears in My tickets and, for the support team (`MANAGE_SUPPORT_TICKETS`), in Support tickets with the category "Demo request". Nothing in the ticket flow changes. | [REQ-SUP-001](../ticket-management/requirement.md) | .10 |

## Compare and cost estimate
| ID | Rule | Enforced in | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-EVL-021 | **Public and read-only.** `GET /products/compare` needs no sign-in, changes nothing and writes no audit entry. A token, when sent, only adds the tax estimate. | `SecurityConfig` (`GET /products/**`), `CompareService` (read-only transaction) | .12 |
| BR-EVL-022 | **Items.** `items` is a comma-separated list of `productId` or `productId:planId` (spaces are ignored, empty parts skipped). **2 to 4** items are required (else 400 "Choose 2 to 4 products or plans to compare."); a part that is not a number or a pair is refused (400 "\"x\" is not a product or a product:plan pair."); the **same item twice** is refused (400 "The same item is chosen twice."); the same product with **different plans** is allowed. | `CompareService#parse` | .12 |
| BR-EVL-023 | **What an item shows.** The product must be **ACTIVE** (else 404 "Product not found: {id}"). A chosen plan must **belong to the product** (400 "Plan {id} does not belong to {product}."). With **no plan chosen** the item uses the product's **entry plan, the one with the lowest price**; a product **without plans** is shown at its **flat price** with no plan, in **USD**. | `CompareService#item` | .12 |
| BR-EVL-024 | **Seats.** `seats` is a whole number from **1 to 100 000** (default 1; else 400 "Seats must be between 1 and 100000."). It applies to every item. | `CompareService#compare` | .12, .13 |
| BR-EVL-025 | **Cost.** Subtotal = **unit price x seats**, rounded half up to 2 decimals, **in the item's own currency; currencies are never converted** and nothing is added across items. The billing period is the plan's (null without a plan). | `CompareService#item` | .13 |
| BR-EVL-026 | **Tax estimate status**, in this order: **`NOT_SIGNED_IN`** (no token); **`NEEDS_BILLING_DETAILS`** (signed in, but the customer, or for an organization member the organization, has no billing details or no country); **`ESTIMATED`** with tax 0 and total = subtotal when the subtotal is 0; **`AT_CHECKOUT`** when the **external tax provider** is enabled; **`AT_CHECKOUT`** when the EIS tax rules cannot tax the sale (their preview is not `CALCULATED`); otherwise **`ESTIMATED`** with the tax and total (subtotal + tax). | `CompareService#billing`, `#tax` | .13 |
| BR-EVL-027 | **How the tax is worked out.** From the billing country and state, the tax number, the product's **tax class** and the customer type (a member of an organization is a **business** using the organization's billing details; anyone else is an **individual** using their own), on the subtotal in the item's currency, with **EIS's own tax rules** ([REQ-BIL-002](../tax-rules/requirement.md)). It is an **estimate**: checkout calculates the final tax. | `CompareService#tax`, `TaxRuleService#preview` | .13 |
| BR-EVL-028 | **Included features.** The plan's included features (split on new lines, commas and semicolons), else the product's feature tags, else none. Each item also carries the product's `trialDays`. | `CompareService#features` | .13 |

## Worked examples
| Request | Result |
|---|---|
| `items=7:21,9:30&seats=3`, plan 21 costs 100.00 INR, plan 30 costs 250.50 INR | Item 1 subtotal 300.00 INR, item 2 subtotal 751.50 INR; tax `NOT_SIGNED_IN` for a visitor |
| `items=7,9` (product 7 has plans Pro 100.00 and Lite 40.00; product 9 has no plans and a flat price of 10.00) | `seats` 1; item 1 is the Lite plan at 40.00; item 2 has no plan and costs 10.00 USD |
| `items=7` | 400 "Choose 2 to 4 products or plans to compare." |
| `items=7,7` | 400 "The same item is chosen twice." |
| `items=7:21,7:22` | Allowed: two plans of product 7 side by side |
| A product with `trialDays` 7 and plans Basic 100 and Enterprise 900, started by an individual on 1 Oct | ACTIVE trial on Enterprise, 1 seat, `expiresAt` 8 Oct, auto-renew off; the notice "ends soon" goes out from 5 Oct; on 8 Oct the next hourly run expires it |
