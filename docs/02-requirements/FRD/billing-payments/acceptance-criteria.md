# Acceptance criteria — Billing & Payments (REQ-BIL-001)

Source for test cases in `test-cases/functional/billing-payments/`. AC-36 onward are the sprint 2026.4.3 answers ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97)); their test cases are in the last column.

| ID | Requirement | Given / When / Then |
|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | REQ-BIL-001.1 | **Given** a signed-in customer **when** they save billing details with a blank billing name or an invalid billing email **then** the save is refused with a field error; **when** all required fields are valid **then** the details are saved and shown on reload. |
| AC-2 | REQ-BIL-001.2, .3 | **Given** a paid plan **when** a subscription starts, renews, or an organization order is approved **then** an OPEN invoice is created with a unique invoice number, one line per plan, the billing period and the plan's currency. |
| AC-3 | REQ-BIL-001.4, BR-1 | **Given** two customers **when** customer A requests customer B's invoice **then** the response is 404. |
| AC-4 | REQ-BIL-001.5, .6 | **Given** an OPEN invoice and a configured gateway **when** the customer completes Razorpay Checkout and the signature verifies **then** the payment is CAPTURED and the invoice is PAID. |
| AC-5 | REQ-BIL-001.5, BR-5 | **Given** a checkout result with an invalid signature **when** the backend verifies it **then** nothing changes and an error is returned. |
| AC-6 | REQ-BIL-001.7 | **Given** a failed payment **when** the customer returns to the invoice **then** it is still OPEN, the failure reason is shown, and **Retry payment** is available. |
| AC-7 | REQ-BIL-001.8, BR-6 | **Given** a `payment.captured` webhook delivered twice with the same event ID **when** both are received **then** the payment is updated once and both deliveries get a success response. |
| AC-8 | REQ-BIL-001.8 | **Given** a webhook with an invalid signature **when** it is received **then** it is rejected and nothing changes. |
| AC-9 | REQ-BIL-001.10, BR-9 | **Given** a customer adding a card **when** they have not ticked the saving consent **then** Save card is disabled; **when** the card is saved **then** the list shows network, last 4 digits and expiry, and no full card number exists anywhere in EIS. |
| AC-10 | REQ-BIL-001.10, BR-8 | **Given** two saved methods **when** the customer sets one as default **then** it is the only default; **when** they remove a method **then** it disappears and its token is deleted at Razorpay. |
| AC-11 | REQ-BIL-001.11 | **Given** a PAID invoice **when** the customer downloads it **then** both the invoice and the receipt documents are available; an OPEN invoice offers the invoice document only. |

| AC-15 | REQ-BIL-001.15 | **Given** an INR invoice **then** amounts are shown with the INR symbol and formatting from the platform currency settings. |
| AC-16 | REQ-BIL-001.16 | **Given** paid invoices this period **when** the organization admin opens the business dashboard **then** the spending card shows the total paid instead of "not available". |
| AC-17 | REQ-BIL-001.17 | **Given** any payment, refund, reconcile, payment-method or billing-details change **then** an audit entry records the action and the acting user. |
| AC-18 | Accessibility | **Given** every Billing screen and dialog **then** it passes the automated accessibility (axe) test. |
| AC-19 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** a customer at checkout step 2 with the gateway configured **when** they select Credit / debit card, tick the terms checkbox and click Pay **then** Razorpay Checkout opens with the card method preselected for the invoice total, and on verified success step 3 shows "Payment successful" with the invoice number and method. |
| AC-20 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** step 2 **when** the customer selects UPI (or Other online methods) and clicks Pay **then** Razorpay Checkout opens with that method preselected (Other: methods per Open question 14). |
| AC-21 | REQ-BIL-001.18, BR-BIL-001 | **Given** the card option is expanded **then** the card number, expiry and CVV shown on the EIS page are read-only placeholders; **and** no EIS frontend state, request payload, log, database column or analytics event contains a card number, expiry or CVV at any point of the flow. |
| AC-22 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** step 2 with any option selected **when** the terms checkbox is not ticked **then** Pay / Generate invoice is disabled; **when** it is ticked **then** the button is enabled. |

| AC-24 | REQ-BIL-001.14, .18 | **Given** no Razorpay credentials **when** the customer reaches step 2 **then** card, UPI and other online options are disabled with the banner "Online payments are not available yet.", and Pay by invoice is still available if the customer is allowed to use it (Open question 9). |
| AC-25 | REQ-BIL-001.19 | **Given** a customer allowed to pay by invoice **when** they select Pay by invoice, tick the terms checkbox and click Generate invoice **then** the invoice is finalized with route OFFLINE and status OPEN, step 3 shows the invoice number, total, bank details and "Quote invoice number <number> as the payment reference", and the invoice is emailed to the billing email. |
| AC-26 | REQ-BIL-001.19 | **Given** a customer not allowed to pay by invoice **then** the option is not shown and the API refuses the offline route. |
| AC-27 | REQ-BIL-001.20 | **Given** an OPEN invoice with route OFFLINE and open amount 1,220.80 **when** an admin records an offline payment of 1,220.80 with a method, a date not in the future and a reference number **then** the invoice is PAID, a payment with method type OFFLINE exists, the receipt is downloadable, and an audit entry names the admin. |

| AC-30 | Accessibility | **Given** the checkout screen, the Record offline payment dialog and the offline settings form **then** each passes the axe test; the payment options form a radio group operable with arrow keys and Space/Enter; the stepper marks the current step with `aria-current="step"`. |
| AC-31 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** the Payment step **then** the billing-details summary shows Contact and Billing address with **Change** links that reopen Billing details; the Amount due badge shows the total; the tiles Card, UPI, Netbanking, Wallets and (if allowed) Pay by invoice are shown in one row; the selected tile is filled with the primary colour. |
| AC-32 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** Netbanking or Wallets is selected **when** the customer ticks the terms checkbox and clicks Pay **then** Razorpay Checkout opens with that method preselected. |

| AC-34 | REQ-BIL-001.18 | **Given** a payment in progress **then** the footer's **Complete order** button is disabled; **when** the payment is confirmed **then** it moves to the Complete step; clicking it never starts a second payment. |
