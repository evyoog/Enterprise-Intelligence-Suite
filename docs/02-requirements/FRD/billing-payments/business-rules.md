# Business rules — Billing & Payments (REQ-BIL-001)

Feature-specific rules. Cross-feature rules that also apply: [BR-BIL-001 No raw card data](../../../03-business-rules/BR-BIL-001-no-raw-card-data.md) and [BR-SEC-001 Central secrets file](../../../03-business-rules/BR-SEC-001-central-secrets-file.md).

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-1 | A customer sees only their own billing data; an organization's billing data is visible only to members with the **Manage billing** permission (BR-19). Any other invoice, payment or payment method is refused with a generic 404. |
| BR-2 | An invoice is created in the currency of the plan it bills. Amounts are stored in the currency's smallest unit (for example paise). |
| BR-3 | An invoice number is unique and never changes after the invoice is finalized. A finalized invoice's lines and amounts never change. |
| BR-4 | Only an OPEN invoice can be paid. A PAID or VOID invoice cannot be paid again. |
| BR-5 | A payment is recorded as successful only after the Razorpay payment signature (or webhook signature) is verified with the secret from the common secrets file. An unverified result changes nothing. |
| BR-6 | Each Razorpay webhook event is processed at most once, identified by its event ID. Repeated deliveries are acknowledged and ignored. |
| BR-7 | A refund amount must be greater than zero and not more than the captured amount minus refunds already made. A reason is required. |
| BR-8 | Only one payment method per customer or organization is the default. Removing the default leaves no default until another is chosen. |
| BR-9 | Saving a card requires the customer's explicit consent, recorded with the time it was given. |
| BR-10 | When any Razorpay credential is missing, all Razorpay-dependent actions are refused with "Payment gateway not configured"; nothing is sent to Razorpay. |
| BR-11 | Payments, refunds, reconciles, payment-method changes and billing-detail changes are audited with the acting user. |
| BR-12 | **Start after payment** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-1). A subscription created by a cart purchase of a **paid** plan is PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION until its invoice is PAID (card, UPI, netbanking, wallet, or an offline payment recorded by an administrator), then ACTIVE with its term starting at that moment. A **free** plan, and a product the customer already has ACTIVE, start at once. A PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION subscription grants no entitlement ([REQ-SUB-002](../entitlements/requirement.md) BR-1). |
| BR-13 | **Pay by invoice and approved organization orders start at once** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-1). Choosing Pay by invoice on an OPEN invoice (BR-18) activates the subscriptions on it immediately, with the invoice's due date; a subscription created by an approved organization order is ACTIVE from approval. |
| BR-14 | **Activation is idempotent.** The invoice publishes an activation event (reason `PAID` or `PAY_BY_INVOICE`); the subscription module activates only subscriptions still PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION. The same payment confirmed twice (checkout and webhook, or a repeated webhook) changes nothing the second time, so the start date is not reset. |
| BR-15 | **A tax-blocked Draft keeps the purchase pending.** A Draft invoice (tax could not be determined, [REQ-BIL-002](../tax-rules/requirement.md)) cannot be paid and has no payment route, so its subscription stays PENDING_SUBSCRIPTION; after Retry tax issues the invoice, the normal rules apply. |
| BR-16 | **Invoice number and terms** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-2): `PREFIX/YY-YY/NNNNNN`, financial year April to March, six-digit sequence restarting each financial year (for example `EIS/26-27/000123`); due date = issue date + payment terms, default 15 days. Settings: Billing settings → Business & invoicing. An issued number never changes (BR-3). |
| BR-17 | **Reminder schedule** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-2). For each OPEN invoice with a due date: `BEFORE_DUE` when the due date is 1 to 3 days away, `ON_DUE` on the due date (to 6 days after), `AFTER_DUE` from 7 days after. Each kind is sent **once** per invoice (table `invoice_reminder`, one row per invoice and kind). If the job missed days, only the most advanced kind that applies is sent. Paid, refunded, void and Draft invoices and invoices without a due date get none. Recipients: the individual owner, or the organization's administrators; the first recipient's message goes to the billing e-mail on the invoice. |
| BR-18 | **Who may pay by invoice** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-4). Allowed only when (a) the global Pay by invoice switch in Billing settings is on **and** (b) the paying owner is an organization the platform administrator has enabled. An individual is never allowed. Otherwise the checkout hides the tile, `payByInvoiceAllowed` is false and the offline route is refused with 403 `PAY_BY_INVOICE_NOT_ALLOWED`. Enabling or disabling an organization is audited (`ORG_PAY_BY_INVOICE_ENABLED` / `ORG_PAY_BY_INVOICE_DISABLED`); setting the value it already has changes and audits nothing. |
| BR-19 | **Organization billing permission** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-5). Every call under `/organization/me/billing/**`, reads included, requires an ACTIVE member of an organization in good standing who holds `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION_BILLING`; otherwise 403 "You do not have permission to do this". Organization admins hold it by default; it can be given to or removed from a member individually (REQ-TEN-005). `MANAGE_ORGANIZATION` alone no longer opens billing. |
| BR-20 | **Policy page links** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-7). Terms, Privacy and Refund URLs are optional, start with `http://` or `https://`, and are linked in the checkout consent sentence and printed on invoices (each only if set). They are not secrets. |
| BR-21 | **Active gateway** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-8). Exactly one registered gateway is active (`billing_settings.active_gateway`, default `RAZORPAY`). Only a gateway whose credentials are set can be made active (400 otherwise); an unknown code is 404. Switching affects new payments only; a payment already started stays with the gateway that created it. Changing it is audited (`BILLING_ACTIVE_GATEWAY_CHANGED`). The gateway list never carries a secret; credentials come only from the backend environment or secrets file (BR-SEC-001). |
| BR-22 | **Purchase-order number** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-9). Optional; only the invoice's owner may set it (another owner's invoice is a 404); allowed only while the invoice is OPEN or a Draft (409 once paid); up to 50 characters; blank clears it; printed on the invoice; every change audited (`INVOICE_PO_NUMBER_SET`). |
| BR-23 | **Refunds, bank payments, fees** ([C97](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c97) BI-9). Only platform administrators refund, full or partial within BR-7. A recorded offline (bank) payment must equal the open amount; no partial bank payments. No fees line exists on invoices or the checkout summary. |
| BR-24 | **Coupon definition** ([C100](../../../01-business/roadmap/open-decisions.md#c100) PB-1 note). Code: 3 to 40 characters, letters, digits, `-` and `_`, starting with a letter or digit, stored in capitals, unique (409 if it exists). Description: up to 200 characters. Type `PERCENT`: 1 to 99 (up to 2 decimals); type `FIXED`: an amount above zero in the currency's smallest unit and a three-letter currency. Expiry date and time: required; for a new coupon it must be in the future. The code cannot be changed after creation. | backend (the screen checks first) | .34 |
| BR-25 | **Coupon lifecycle.** A coupon can be edited (everything but its code), disabled before its expiry and enabled again; editing does not change past redemptions. A coupon can be **deleted only if it was never redeemed** (409 "A coupon that has been redeemed cannot be deleted. Disable it instead."). Status is derived: `DISABLED` when not enabled; else `EXPIRED` when the expiry has passed; else `ACTIVE`. Create, update, enable, disable and delete are audited (`COUPON_CREATED`, `COUPON_UPDATED`, `COUPON_ENABLED`, `COUPON_DISABLED`, `COUPON_DELETED`). | backend | .34 |
| BR-26 | **A coupon can be used** only when: the code exists after trimming and capitalizing (else "This coupon code is not valid."); it is enabled ("This coupon is not active."); its expiry has not passed ("This coupon has expired."); a fixed coupon's currency is the cart's or invoice's ("This coupon is for {currency} purchases only."); and its discount is less than the subtotal ("This coupon is worth more than your cart. Add more to your cart to use it."), so a coupon never takes a cart to zero. One code per cart and per invoice; codes do not stack. | backend | .35 |
| BR-27 | **Discount and tax.** Percent: subtotal × percentage ÷ 100 rounded half up; fixed: the amount. The discount is shared across the lines in proportion to their amounts, the last line taking the rounding remainder and no line more than its own amount. Tax is calculated on each line's amount **minus** its share. `invoice.subtotal` = gross − discount; `invoice.total` = `subtotal` + tax. The checkout summary's `subtotal` is the **gross** (`invoice.subtotal` + `discountAmount`), so gross − discount + tax = total. | backend | .35 |
| BR-28 | **Redemption.** The code is checked again and the coupon's `redeemedCount` goes up by one when the invoice is created, in the same transaction, once per invoice; if the check fails nothing is created. There is no per-customer or maximum-redemptions limit (Not specified). A coupon is not applied to a renewal invoice, a seat-increase invoice or a purchase request. | backend | .35 |
| BR-29 | **Who manages coupons.** Every `/admin/billing/coupons` call needs the platform permission `MANAGE_BILLING` (401 without a token, 403 without the permission). Customers never read the coupon list: they see only the result of the code they entered. | backend | .34 |
| BR-30 | **Per-seat lines.** An organization subscription's new or renewal invoice line has quantity = seats and amount = unit price × seats; an individual's has quantity 1. A **seat-increase invoice** has one line "Added seats: {product} — {plan}" with quantity = the added seats and unit amount = line amount ÷ quantity; its amount is added seats × seat price × remaining days ÷ days in the term (30 Monthly, 365 Yearly, else the term's length), rounded half up to the smallest unit. Nothing is invoiced for a free plan or zero remaining days. | backend | .36 |
