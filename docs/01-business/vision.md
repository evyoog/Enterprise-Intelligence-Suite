# Vision — eVyoog Enterprise Intelligence Suite (EIS)

eVyoog EIS is a cloud platform, offered as Platform as a Service (PaaS), that hosts multiple AI product suites — **Valam.ai, Varthan.ai, Thittam.ai, Thiran.ai, Yukth.ai, Tharav.ai** — on top of one shared platform layer.

## Why
Every product suite needs the same foundation: identity, security, tenant management, subscriptions, notifications and analytics. Building it once, as a platform, lets each suite focus on its own domain and gives customers one account, one login and one place to manage every eVyoog product they use.

## What success looks like
- An organization registers once, subscribes to any suite, and manages seats and access in one place.
- Users sign in once (Keycloak SSO, optional SAML federation with the customer's own IdP, MFA) and move between suites without logging in again.
- Platform administrators manage the catalog, tenants, roles and privileged access with a full audit trail.
- New product suites plug into the platform without re-implementing identity or billing.
